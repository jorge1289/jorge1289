### About me

I'm a software engineer and grad student working on **robotics perception and autonomous systems, with an emphasis on vehicles**. I'm currently working on my master's thesis in this area.

- 🤖 **Now:** Graduate Research Assistant at SDSU's Computer Systems Intelligence Lab. I ran Isaac ROS Visual SLAM to enable camera-based localization, with no LiDAR, in a Nav2 autonomous navigation pipeline, achieving reliable autonomous goal navigation.
- 💼 **Previously:** SWE intern at **Microsoft** (M365 Copilot) and **Qualcomm** (CPU software infrastructure), and R&D at Berkeley's ACE Lab.
- 🎓 Graduating with my M.S. in **May 2027**.
